How Self-Healing Works

CloudOps detects when the monitored Docker container stops or its health endpoint becomes unavailable. It displays a recovery notification, waits 10 seconds, then attempts to restart the existing container. The system verifies application health and records the recovery result. A maximum of two restart attempts are allowed for a recovery event.
